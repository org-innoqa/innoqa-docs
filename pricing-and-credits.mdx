## How credits work

Humainum uses one shared credit system for AI actions and expert assistance. Pick a plan and add more credits whenever you need them — purchased credits carry over across billing periods.

Every plan lists, at minimum:

- Credits per month
- Concurrent AI tasks
- Projects per workspace

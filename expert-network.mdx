## Bringing an expert into your project

The expert joins the same Humainum environment your agent is already working in — they understand the project context, review what's been built, and help move the work forward.

## Joining as an expert

<Steps>
  <Step title="Apply">
    Submit a headline, bio, skills and portfolio.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get reviewed">
    Every application is reviewed by hand, not auto-approved.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick your tasks">
    Browse open tasks and submit a proposal for the ones that fit. You set your own minimum task size, in credits, so you only see tasks worth your time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deliver & get paid">
    Accepted tasks come with direct access to the project's repository. Once your work is accepted, request a payout by bank transfer, PayPal or Wise.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  What you'll need to apply: a portfolio link or a few examples of your work, a GitHub account (for repository access on accepted tasks), and a clear headline and bio describing what you do.
</Note>

Completed tasks, ratings and review count build a visible profile over time.

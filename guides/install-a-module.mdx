---
title: 'Install your first module'
description: 'Add CRM, bookings, a chatbot and more, only when your project needs them.'
---

The Toolbox is Humainum's connected suite of business modules. Install what a project needs, when it needs it — nothing installs itself upfront.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Browse the Toolbox">
    From your project, open the module list and browse by category — Acquisition, Operations, Conversion, Insight.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a module">
    Check its status first — see [Modules overview](/modules/overview) for what's Available today versus still in Beta.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Install it">
    Installing connects the module to your project's [Business Profile](/modules/overview#core), so it starts from the same facts about your business every other module already uses.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure and go">
    Each module has its own setup — connect a channel, publish availability, whatever that module needs — then it's live.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Humainum is a provider-agnostic AI app builder: you describe what you want, an AI agent plans the work and writes real code into a real GitHub repository, and verified human experts can join your project whenever a task needs a person's judgment.

It isn't a website builder with an AI feature bolted on. Three things work together on every project.

## What you can build

Business websites, booking platforms, SaaS products, internal tools, marketplaces and digital services — for the web or as <a href="/native-mobile-apps">native iOS and Android apps</a>. As Humainum grows, the range of products and integrations grows with it.

## No lock-in

Every project is a real repository from day one — inspect it, clone it, self-host it, or connect your own AI provider to it.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need to know how to code?">
    No. Humainum is designed for people who know what they want to create, not necessarily how to build it. Describe the outcome and Humainum guides you through the rest.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What can I build with Humainum?">
    From business websites and booking platforms to SaaS products, internal tools, marketplaces and digital services. As Humainum grows, the range of products and integrations grows with it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I import a project I already have?">
    Yes. Connect an existing web or mobile project, verify ownership, and continue building on the real code with AI and the team — no need to start over.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why would I need a human expert if AI builds the product?">
    Because not every decision is a generation problem. Architecture, design judgment, unusual integrations, positioning and complex requirements sometimes benefit from experience. When that happens, Humainum lets the right expert join your existing project instead of sending you somewhere else.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do experts work with my project?">
    Experts join the same Humainum environment your AI is already working in. They understand the project context, review what's been built and help move the work forward.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do credits work?">
    Humainum uses a shared credit system for supported AI actions and expert assistance. Choose a plan and add more credits whenever you need them — purchased credits carry over across billing periods.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I own what I build?">
    Yes. Every project keeps a real, portable GitHub repository from day one — build, self-host, or move it anywhere. Ownership, hosting and any third-party licensing terms are shown clearly before you publish or connect external services.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is my project data isolated from other users?">
    Yes. Every project gets its own PostgreSQL schema with scoped access, and GitHub, database and API key access are protected per project.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can Humainum help after my product launches?">
    That's one of the main reasons Humainum exists. Use modules for SEO, advertising, content, CRM, bookings, payments and more without rebuilding your workflow around a collection of separate tools.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is Humainum just another AI website builder?">
    No. The builder is only the beginning. Humainum connects AI development, verified human expertise and business modules inside one continuous experience.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
